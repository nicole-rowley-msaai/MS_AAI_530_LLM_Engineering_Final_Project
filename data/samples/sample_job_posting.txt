Junior Data Analyst
Brightline Retail Group | San Diego, CA | Full-time

About the role
We are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join our merchandising analytics
team. You will turn sales and inventory data into reports and dashboards that
help store leaders make decisions.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain weekly and monthly sales reports.
- Write SQL queries to pull data from our data warehouse.
- Design Tableau dashboards for store and regional managers.
- Present findings to non-technical stakeholders.
- Lead small analysis projects from question to recommendation.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree in business, statistics, or a related field.
- Proficiency in SQL.
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI.
- Advanced Excel skills.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Nice to have
- Experience with Python or R.
- Retail or e-commerce experience.
- Experience leading a team or project.
